# rMQR Code (`breader-qr`, milestone M12)

rMQR (Rectangular Micro QR Code) is read by the QR reader (`QrReader`): the finder scan is shared, so a frame pays for one scan. A result carries `Symbology::Rmqr`; it can be switched off with `ScanOptions::symbologies` independently of QR Code and Micro QR Code. Like QR it works on the binary plane in both polarities (`uses_binary() == true`, `handles_inversion() == true`) and also looks at the luma image for a retry. Part of the cross-cutting [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md); status and spec references are in the [symbology index](README.md); the QR design it builds on is [qr.md](qr.md), its sibling [microqr.md](microqr.md). Decisions: [D-039](../decisions.md#d-039).

## Scope

In scope: all 32 versions R7x43 to R17x139 (heights 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17; widths 27, 43, 59, 77, 99, 139), error correction levels M and H, numeric, alphanumeric, byte and kanji modes, ECI, FNC1 in first and second position, any rotation, perspective, light-on-dark and mirrored symbols. Out of scope: Structured Append (rMQR has none). Normative source: ISO/IEC 23941. The standard text was not available while writing this; the tables were written from memory and checked against the black-box output of an encoder (`qrcode-generator` with the `rmqr` feature, a dev-dependency of `breader-qr` and a dependency of `tools/synth`; its source was not read) and the real symbols of the zxing-cpp samples (`rmqrcode-1`: `R7x43-H`, its inverted version and `R17x139`). Where the two disagree the real symbols win.

## Modules and data flow

Code lives in `crates/breader-qr/src/rmqr/`: `spec` (sizes, alignment columns, block layouts of the 64 symbol types, character count widths, format words, the function-pattern template and the data module order of each version), `decode` (format information from both copies, unmasking, de-interleaving, Reed-Solomon), `bits` (segments, ECI, FNC1, padding), `reader` (orchestration, edge fitting, sampling and gates), `tests` and `reader_tests`.

One pass of `QrReader::scan`: the finder search (shared, both polarities) -> QR triplets as before -> for each polarity, the finders not inside a decoded QR, Micro QR or rMQR quad become candidates, strongest first, capped by effort (4, 10 and 40 at Fast, Balanced and Exhaustive, the Micro QR caps). One fit of the finder pose per candidate serves both Micro QR and rMQR.

## Stages

1. **Candidates and orientation.** The finder (7x7, top left of the symbol) is fitted as for Micro QR (pose by template, then edges). Eight frames are tried: four rotations, mirrored or not. In each, the outer edges of the finder are located as boundaries (three rounds, because a point found on an edge is only as good as the prediction along it), then the first ten boundaries of the top timing row (dark to light at module 7, then alternating) are tracked with a refit after each; fewer than six found rejects the orientation.
2. **Version.** The left copy of the format information (18 modules: three columns of five at columns 8 to 10 and rows 1 to 5, plus column 11 rows 1 to 3) is read through that frame and decoded: BCH(18,6), generator `0x1F25`, XOR `0x1FAB2`, the 6-bit value is the level (bit 5: 0 M, 1 H) and the version indicator (bits 0 to 4, version order R7x43, R7x59, ..., R17x139). Distance 0 or 1 is trusted and names the size. A word at distance 2 or 3, or no word, is followed (Balanced and Exhaustive) by the sizes whose width the end of the top timing row implies (alternating boundaries, three dark modules at each alignment column and at the right end), so a damaged left copy is recovered through the right copy later.
3. **Mapping.** For the named size the boundaries of known timing modules are listed: the top and bottom rows, the left and right columns, the vertical timing column of each alignment pattern, the finder, plus (optional, they need a light quiet zone) the outer edges of the dark edge modules. They are located progressively from the finder outwards (each timing line predicts its next edge from its own last ones when the mapping misses; a refit every six), then in three rounds over all of them with a shrinking window. The mapping is a homography, or an affine map when that explains the points about as well (a long thin symbol leaves the perspective terms to noise). Fewer than 75 % of the mandatory boundaries found, or eight misses in a row, rejects the size.
4. **Sampling and gates (before Reed-Solomon).** Both quiet rings (half and one and a half modules outside) must be light in 75 % of their in-image samples. The grid is sampled (majority of five sub-samples from 3 px per module). Required against the template of the size: finder and separators (up to 64 modules) with at most 6 wrong, all other function modules (timing, corner patterns, alignment, corner sub-finder) with at most 10 % wrong.
5. **Format and error correction.** Both copies of the format information are read from the grid (the right copy sits at columns `W-8..W-6`, rows `H-6..H-2`, the three extra bits in row `H-6`, columns `W-5..W-3`; XOR `0x20A7B`). Each decodes within distance 3; if both decode they must name the same value, or one must be within 1 and at least 2 nearer than the other; a single copy is accepted only within 2. The version must equal the sampled size. Data modules in two-module columns from the right (the first pair is columns `W-2` and `W-3`, the outer columns are timing), upwards then downwards, skipping function modules, are unmasked with the one fixed pattern (`(row/2 + column/3)` even flips), cut into codewords, de-interleaved into the blocks of the size and level (the last `data mod blocks` blocks one codeword longer) and corrected over GF(256) as for QR. Misdecode guard: errors corrected per block `<= (ec - reserve) / 2` with reserve 2 up to 8 check codewords and 1 above.
6. **Bitstream.** Mode indicator of 3 bits (000 end, 001 numeric, 010 alphanumeric, 011 byte, 100 kanji, 101 FNC1 first position, 110 FNC1 second position with an 8-bit application indicator, 111 ECI), character count of 2 to 9 bits by version and mode, a terminator of 000 and then zeros to a codeword boundary and alternating 0xEC, 0x11. Anything else (data after the terminator, a wrong pad, reserved modes, an empty segment, no data, FNC1 after data) rejects the symbol.
7. **Results.** `Symbology::Rmqr`, `SymbolMeta::Qr(QrMeta)` with `version` 1 to 32 (the version indicator plus one), `width` and `height`, `ec_level` M or H, `mask` 0, `mirrored`, `eci`. The quad maps the module corners (0,0), (W,0), (W,H), (0,H). AIM identifier `]Q1`, `]Q3` (FNC1 first) or `]Q5` (FNC1 second), plus one when an ECI is present; only `]Q1` is confirmed (by the zxing-cpp result of `R7x43-H`), see [D-039](../decisions.md#d-039).

## Effort

Fast: same finder scan, at most 4 candidates, no retries and no sweep over sizes. Balanced and Exhaustive add: the sweep over the sizes of the width found, and after a grid that passed the structure gates fails to decode, a local-threshold sampling from the luma image. Exhaustive tries up to 40 candidates per pass.

## False-positive control

A finder and a short format word would be a weak gate, so a symbol must pass in order: ten timing boundaries after the finder, a format word within distance 3, the mapping found on the timing patterns of the four edges (75 % of the boundaries), the two quiet rings, the function patterns of the sampled grid (finder and separators, timing on all four edges and at the alignment columns, corner patterns), the format words of both copies agreeing and naming the sampled size, Reed-Solomon with the guard, and a well-formed bitstream with exact padding. Tests: random grids with perfect function patterns and random format and data never decode (200 per version), QR codes of several versions (also with the data area wiped and the other finders occluded), checkerboards, stripes, noise and ring-like blobs with timing-like rows never read as rMQR at any effort ([reader tests](../../crates/breader-qr/src/rmqr/reader_tests.rs)); the regression gates have 0 false positives.

## Evaluation

Gates: `zxing-samples` (`rmqrcode-1`, 3 labels, now counted), the synthetic sets (rMQR images appended after the Micro QR ones: the 64 size and level variants spread over the images, every third image mirrored, every class; the shared sets are regenerated at merge time) and every other gate for the false-positive count. Measurements: [BASELINES](../BASELINES.md#rmqr-reader-2026-10-11). Fuzz target `rmqr_grid`.

## As built

- The block structure (blocks and check codewords per block) of the 64 symbol types, the character count widths and the function patterns were derived and checked as described under scope. Spelled out because the standard text was not at hand: the vertical timing columns at the alignment centres 21 (width 43), 19 and 39 (59), 25 and 51 (77), 23, 49 and 75 (99), 27, 55, 83 and 111 (139) carry 3x3 rings at the top and bottom; the corner patterns are three dark modules along the edge and two on the adjacent edge, with a light module inside (`(1, W-2)` and `(H-2, 1)`, reserved); the corner sub-finder is a 5x5 pattern like a QR alignment pattern at the bottom right.
- The order of the three extra format bits (15 to 17) is the same in both samples whichever way they are stored; the decoder takes the nearer of the two orders.
- Remainder bits (0 to 7 at the end of the data modules) are ignored.
- The boundaries of a binarized edge are only known to about half a pixel and dark modules bloom by a similar amount, so the outer-edge boundaries (dark module against quiet zone) enter the fit with the same weight as the timing boundaries and are optional.

## Known gaps

- Small symbols of 2 px per module are read only after the up-scale retry (Balanced and up); at Fast `R7x43-H.png` and its inverted version are not read.
- Only the two real symbol types of the zxing-cpp set confirm the tables against an independent source; the other 62 rest on the memory-plus-black-box check described above.
- No quiet-zone-free photograph or strongly curved print has been seen; none is expected to read, the quiet rings are required when inside the image.
- AIM modifiers `]Q3` and `]Q5` and the ECI increments are unverified.
